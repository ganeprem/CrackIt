ABOUT CRACKIT

Helping students prepare smarter

CrackIt is a mock-test platform designed to help aspirants prepare effectively for competitive examinations.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide students with high-quality mock examinations that closely resemble real competitive exams. CrackIt helps students understand their strengths, identify weak areas and improve their examination strategy through detailed performance analysis.

Why CrackIt?

Preparing for competitive examinations requires more than simply studying concepts. Students need regular practice under examination conditions. CrackIt provides timed mock examinations, detailed solutions, performance analytics and ranking information so students can continuously improve their preparation.

Exams We Support

CrackIt is being developed to support major competitive examinations including GATE, JEE, NEET, CAT, banking examinations, SSC and other entrance and recruitment examinations.
